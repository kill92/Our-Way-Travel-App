# Privacy Policy – Our Way Travel (הדרך שלנו)

Last updated: 2 November 2026
Contact: liad2009@gmail.com

## English

Our Way Travel is a private travel-planning app for personal use by a small group of people.

**What the app stores**
- Your trips, lists, notes and settings are stored on your device.
- If you sign in with Google, a backup of this data is saved to a private, hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive (scope `drive.appdata`). Only this app can read it, and you can delete it at any time from your Google Drive settings.
- The app also requests your email address (scope `userinfo.email`) only to identify the signed-in account.
- The encrypted vault is encrypted on your device with a password you choose. We cannot read it or recover the password.

**What is sent to our server (Cloudflare Worker)**
- AI guide questions, place/weather/flight/currency lookups, and sign-in token exchange are sent to a Cloudflare Worker, which forwards them to the relevant service (for example Anthropic, Google, OpenStreetMap, AeroDataBox).
- The Worker only serves signed-in, allow-listed accounts. We do not sell data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with third parties except those needed to answer your request.

**Retention and deletion**
- Delete a backup from the in-app cloud backups list or from Google Drive settings. Revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

**Children**
- The app is not directed to children.
